# ROOT V2 — Community Code of Conduct

We welcome practical, evidence-based discussion about software engineering, UX, healthcare operations and revenue intelligence. Treat contributors and reviewers with respect; critique work and ideas, not people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, spam and disclosure of private data are not permitted.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit participation, and escalate serious concerns. Report conduct concerns privately to info@rootrcm.com; do not publish identifying details or patient information. Project owners retain final moderation decisions. This policy applies to repository issues, PRs, discussions, and associated project spaces.
