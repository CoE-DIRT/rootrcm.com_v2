# Sponsorship and funding readiness

A GitHub Sponsors button is appropriate only after the organization's eligibility, verified sponsor profile and payout recipient are confirmed. Do not seed placeholder usernames or third-party donation URLs as if they were live.

**To activate later:** set up an approved sponsor destination, record internal approval and financial ownership, then add `.github/FUNDING.yml` with only verified provider handles/URLs and turn on `Sponsor button` under repository Settings > General. Review tax, company ownership, conflicts and local payment/eligibility rules before offering sponsorship.

Until then, track partnership opportunities through approved company channels and do not present unverified sponsorship offerings to the public.
