# 00 — Product Blueprint

**Discover → Convert → Operate**, connected by identity, organization, entitlement, payments, CRM and analytics. One app, not disjointed brochure pages.

## Ideal buyers
| Buyer | Fear | ROOT demonstration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice owner | Outsourcing becomes a black box | Audit trail, ownership, aging A/R |
| Practice manager | Staff and EHR change fatigue | Modular, validated migration |
| RCM director / CFO | Missed follow-up, bad cash flow | Payer/specialty/state analysis |
| Credentialing lead | Enrollment delays | Readiness checklist and payer tracking |
| MSO leadership | Fragmentation and vendor lock-in | Scope-controlled operating partner |

## Information architecture
Why ROOT · Services · Technology · Plans · Resources · Company, one responsive public navigation. Customer and employee portals use separate role-aware application shell.

## Lifecycle
Discover → Assess → Scope → Onboard → Connect → Execute → Monitor → Optimize.

## Commercial entry
Free account, free consultation, fixed-fee diagnostic, recurring plan, custom RCM/MSO. No patient information in conversion flows. Screenshot/sample claims do not establish results.
