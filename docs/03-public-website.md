# 03 — Public Website

Ten-section design baseline: Header; interactive hero; pain point selector; services grid; eight-stage timeline; integrations directory; verified trust; ROI or case results; in-house vs outsourcing vs ROOT comparison; four-column footer. Progressive disclosure over endless homepage repetition.

Canonical destinations include specialties and payer/state relationships, full credentialing/licensing, technology DIRT preview, 20 testimonial editorial slots, 10 case studies, 10 success stories, 5 certification evidence slots, 5 partnership evidence slots, founder message, team, jobs, referrals, official CMS forms, templates, social and policy pages.

Any unverified claim is **Pending verification**; do not present a software vendor as a certified partner without authorization.
