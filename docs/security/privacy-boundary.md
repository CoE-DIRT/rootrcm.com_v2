# Public health-data safety

All marketing forms, lead capture and free resource gates are **business contact only**; do not request patient demographic details. A HIPAA compliance claim needs counsel/security evidence; HIPAA is not a universal certification badge. Patient/claim data handling is out of scope until contracts, technical controls, BAAs and architecture sign-off.

Review rate limiting, server validation, audit trails, app-user role/tenant isolation, encryption, consent-gated telemetry and breach response. All examples use synthetic organizations and no real patient identifiers.
